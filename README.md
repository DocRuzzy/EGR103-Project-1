# EGR103-Project-1

## Problem Statement

Design a cardboard carrier for 25 kg grain bags.

## Design Goals

- Safely support one 25 kg grain bag without tearing or collapsing
- Make the bag easier to lift, carry, and move
- Use low-cost corrugated cardboard and simple assembly methods
- Keep the design practical for classroom prototyping

## Proposed Carrier Concept

The carrier is a reinforced corrugated cardboard tray with side walls and built-in hand holds. A double-layer base spreads the weight of the grain bag, while folded side panels increase stiffness and help keep the bag centered during transport.

## Key Requirements

- **Load capacity:** Must hold at least 25 kg
- **Base strength:** Double-layer bottom panel to reduce bending
- **Handles:** Cutout handles on both sides for two-handed lifting
- **Retention:** Side walls to prevent the bag from sliding out
- **Material:** Heavy-duty corrugated cardboard

## Suggested Build Features

1. Use a flat rectangular base sized slightly larger than the grain bag footprint.
2. Fold side panels upward and lock the corners with tabs or glue.
3. Add a second cardboard layer under the base for reinforcement.
4. Reinforce handle areas with extra cardboard patches.
5. Test the carrier with a full 25 kg load and inspect for sagging or tearing.

## Success Criteria

The design is successful if it can carry a 25 kg grain bag securely, can be lifted by hand without failure, and remains simple enough to manufacture from cardboard.
